what the **COMIMa app** offers, available today for Android. It is **free**,
has no ads, and works **offline**.

## A path, with levels

The app does not throw exercises at you in a heap. Each **topic** of the
course has its summary sheet, then **steps** of increasing difficulty, arranged
in **levels**: you start with *Discover*, move on to *Practise*, and so on.
Each theme has its colour and icon — number theory, geometry, combinatorics,
algebra — so you know at a glance what it is about.

The home screen always suggests the **next step**: the topic you started, or
the starting point if you are new.

![Home: level, XP, topic to continue, then the levels in order](/uploads/application-comima-01.jpg)

## Graded instantly, even a formula

True/false and multiple choice are familiar. Here you can also **write your
answer**: a number, a set, an interval, an expression. If your result is right
in another form — *n*(*n*+1)/2 or (*n*² + *n*)/2 — it is accepted,
because the two are equal. And a malformed entry is **never marked wrong**: the
app explains what is off, without taking a point away.

There are also **proofs to put back in order** and fill-in-the-blank texts.
After each answer, you know straight away whether it is right. Answers and
solutions are never shown to students: you search, you get it wrong, you try
again. They are reserved for coaches.

## The library too, offline

The **library** carries the website's exercises with their statements: over a
thousand today, for you to work out yourself. It also holds the course,
competition papers, news and the simulations, which open on the website.

## See your progress, without pressure

Success by theme and by topic, practice streak, activity over the last two
weeks: the **statistics** show where you stand.

To keep the rhythm, you can turn on a **reminder** — at most one a day, at the
time you choose, never if you have already practised, with a pause available
during exams. It stays off until you ask for it.

![Statistics: answers, success, streaks, breakdown by theme](/uploads/application-comima-04.jpg)

## One phone, several profiles

A phone can serve several people: each has their own profile and progress. At
creation you choose **student** or **coach** — one or the other — then give
your school, region and COMIMa centre. This information is only used for
coaching; it is never published.

## Install in three steps

1. Download the file from [comima.mg/app](/en/app/): the page picks the one
   that suits your phone (Android 7 or later).
2. Open it; Android asks to allow installing from the browser. That is normal:
   the app does not come from a store.
3. Open COMIMa. Everything is already inside, nothing more to download.

If the install fails with the first file, take another file from the page: the
“universal” file works everywhere. **Updates** are offered by the app itself,
and every file is verified before it is installed.

![The “About” page: the QR code to show a friend, the website and the partners](/uploads/application-comima-05.jpg)

## An account, if you wish

**Accounts** (Google, Facebook or e-mail) are **optional**: without one,
everything works. With an account, your progress follows you to another phone,
and **to the website**: the [Training](/en/training/) page offers the same
quizzes, graded by the same engine, and the same sign-in. What you do on one
shows up on the other.

## What's next?

Follow-up by your coach comes next, along with **exercise suggestions from
coaches**, with their solution, to enrich the library. A question that looks
wrong or unclear? Tell your coach: that is how the app gets better.

Happy practising!
