On **17 October 2026**, in a classroom of **Ambatondrazaka's lycée**, maths
teachers sat around the same tables as COMIMa's leaders. On the agenda:
**preparing the launch of the club** in Ambatondrazaka, organising the group
sessions, and deciding together how to welcome the students.

![Ambatondrazaka's teachers and the COMIMa team, gathered at the lycée](/uploads/ambatondrazaka-2026-01.jpg)

## An eagerness you could see

What stood out in this information session was the teachers' **enthusiasm**.
They did not come to listen to a presentation: they came to say they wanted to
**be part of the team**. Welcoming students, bringing them together, getting
them to search and to grow: they showed they were ready to commit to it.

![The information session, around the classroom tables](/uploads/ambatondrazaka-2026-02.jpg)

## The headteacher's support

Thank you to the **headteacher (proviseur) of Ambatondrazaka's lycée**, who
took part in the meeting and pledged unconditional help with the
**administration and the premises**, so that the group sessions run under the
best conditions. Without a school that opens its doors, none of this would be
possible.

![In front of the lycée building, with the headteacher and the teachers](/uploads/ambatondrazaka-2026-03.jpg)

## A land of mathematicians

Ambatondrazaka has a reputation as a **breeding ground of talent**: some of
Madagascar's best mathematicians come from there. With teachers this motivated
and a lycée this welcoming, the region's future maths stars have every reason
to be impatient.

![Ambatondrazaka's team, ready to welcome the students](/uploads/ambatondrazaka-2026-04.jpg)

After Antsiranana, [where the group sessions have already begun](/en/news/regroupements-antsiranana-octobre-2026/),
it is now Ambatondrazaka's turn to get going.
