A week ago, Ambatondrazaka's teachers sat around a table to
[prepare the launch of the club](/en/news/ambatondrazaka-seance-information-octobre-2026/).
Today, their classroom is full of students: the **first group session** has
taken place.

![Ambatondrazaka's students gathered on the steps of the lycée](/uploads/ambatondrazaka-regroupement-2026-01.jpg)

## A team that does not dawdle

Between the meeting and the first session: **seven days**. No waiting, no long
hesitation: the teachers said they were in, and they kept their word. Quick
decisions, a room ready, students called together. That is the temperament of
a team eager to move forward.

![A mentor smiles, surrounded by equally delighted students](/uploads/ambatondrazaka-regroupement-2026-03.jpg)

## Students already in the swing

In the yellow-walled room, students took their seats, notebooks open, eyes on
the mentor. On their faces, curiosity and plenty of smiles: you can feel that
this first meeting makes them want to come back.

![A full classroom, every eye on the mentor](/uploads/ambatondrazaka-regroupement-2026-02.jpg)

## Good luck

Good luck to the whole Ambatondrazaka team, mentors and students alike. With
Antsiranana and [Fianarantsoa](/en/news/premier-regroupement-fianarantsoa-octobre-2026/),
the map of group sessions keeps filling in, centre after centre.
