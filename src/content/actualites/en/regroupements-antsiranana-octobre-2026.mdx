They did not wait. On **9 October 2026**, in Antsiranana, the room filled
with students, open notebooks and eyes turned toward the board: this year's
**group sessions** have begun.

On the board, a few words in chalk set the tone: *COMIMa, Madagascar
Mathematical Olympiad Club*. In front of it, a mentor talks with their hands,
the way one does when an idea deserves to be truly shared.

![The mentor at the board, students hanging on every explanation](/uploads/regroupements-antsiranana-2026-01.jpg)

## Faces that are thinking

What strikes you in these photos is not the walls or the wooden benches, it
is the **faces**: chin in hand, brow furrowed, and then the smile of the one
who has just understood. Nobody here is sitting through a lecture. They
search, they doubt, they come back to it.

![Smiling students turned toward the person speaking to them](/uploads/regroupements-antsiranana-2026-02.jpg)

## Mentors and students, the same passion

The mentors are volunteers, and they are here for the same reason as the
students: **they love mathematics**. That love travels better than any
method. It shows in the way a question is asked, in letting people search,
and in leaning over the notebook of someone who is stuck.

![Students listening to the mentor, notebooks open in front of them](/uploads/regroupements-antsiranana-2026-03.jpg)

![Attention and concentration throughout the room](/uploads/regroupements-antsiranana-2026-04.jpg)

## Search, then search again

After listening comes the pencil. The room goes quiet, heads bend over the
sheets, and the mentor moves from desk to desk to offer a hint, never the
answer.

![The mentor leaning over the students' work](/uploads/regroupements-antsiranana-2026-05.jpg)

![A whole room absorbed in one problem](/uploads/regroupements-antsiranana-2026-06.jpg)

![At the back of the room, the board keeps the day's exercises](/uploads/regroupements-antsiranana-2026-07.jpg)

## Whose turn is next?

Good luck to the whole Antsiranana team, mentors and students alike: you are
giving the year its first push. **The other centres will soon join you.** In
Antananarivo and Ambositra too, rooms will soon hum with the same passion.
