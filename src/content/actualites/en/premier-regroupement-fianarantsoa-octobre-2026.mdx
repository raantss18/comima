Fianarantsoa, the **city of scholars of Madagascar**, has joined the COMIMa
community, and it did not dawdle. On **Saturday 24 October 2026**, the
**first group session** for students took place in a large room packed with
maths enthusiasts.

![A room full of students, attentive, pen in hand](/uploads/fianarantsoa-2026-01.jpg)

## A room that breathes mathematics

Mentors and students share the **same passion**, and it shows: eyes follow
the board, pens move, hands go up to ask a question. One student takes the
microphone to answer in front of everyone, another searches, chin in hand.

![A student speaks into the microphone, facing the mentor](/uploads/fianarantsoa-2026-04.jpg)

## A team on the front line

In front of the board, where you can read *Animath – COMIMa*, the mentors
introduced themselves to the students: a team of volunteers who came to pass
on their love of mathematics. Then each of them picked up the chalk, the
microphone or the projector to share their ideas.

![The team of mentors introduces itself to the students](/uploads/fianarantsoa-2026-02.jpg)

![At the board, a mentor explains in front of the whole room](/uploads/fianarantsoa-2026-03.jpg)

![A mentor presents a problem projected on the board](/uploads/fianarantsoa-2026-07.jpg)

## And we move too

A good session also knows how to breathe: at one point the whole room stood
up, **arms in the air**, to stretch before diving back into the problems. The
smiles on these students are worth any speech.

![The whole room on its feet, arms raised](/uploads/fianarantsoa-2026-05.jpg)

![Focused students, row after row](/uploads/fianarantsoa-2026-06.jpg)

![A mentor writes at the board, the room following every move](/uploads/fianarantsoa-2026-08.jpg)

## The movement spreads

After [Antsiranana](/en/news/regroupements-antsiranana-octobre-2026/) and the
teachers' meeting in
[Ambatondrazaka](/en/news/ambatondrazaka-seance-information-octobre-2026/),
it is now **Fianarantsoa**'s turn to launch its group sessions. Welcome to
everyone joining the adventure.
