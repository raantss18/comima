COMIMa in your pocket: the Android app is here

25 October 2026

A maths problem waiting in your phone, even when the network is down: that is what the COMIMa app offers, available today for Android. It is free, has no ads, and works offline.

⬇ Download the COMIMa app (Android)

Free · works offline · Android 7 or newer

To install it, one place: comima.mg/app. From another screen, just scan this code.

A path, with levels

The app does not throw exercises at you in a heap. Each topic of the course has its summary sheet, then steps of increasing difficulty, arranged in levels: you start with Discover, move on to Practise, and so on. Each theme has its colour and icon — number theory, geometry, combinatorics, algebra — so you know at a glance what it is about.

The home screen always suggests the next step: the topic you started, or the starting point if you are new.

Graded instantly, even a formula

True/false and multiple choice are familiar. Here you can also write your answer: a number, a set, an interval, an expression. If your result is right in another form — n(n+1)/2 or (n² + n)/2 — it is accepted, because the two are equal. And a malformed entry is never marked wrong: the app explains what is off, without taking a point away.

There are also proofs to put back in order and fill-in-the-blank texts. After each answer, you know straight away whether it is right. Answers and solutions are never shown to students: you search, you get it wrong, you try again. They are reserved for coaches.

The library too, offline

The library carries the website’s exercises with their statements: over a thousand today, for you to work out yourself. It also holds the course, competition papers, news and the simulations, which open on the website.

See your progress, without pressure

Success by theme and by topic, practice streak, activity over the last two weeks: the statistics show where you stand.

To keep the rhythm, you can turn on a reminder — at most one a day, at the time you choose, never if you have already practised, with a pause available during exams. It stays off until you ask for it.

One phone, several profiles

A phone can serve several people: each has their own profile and progress. At creation you choose student or coach — one or the other — then give your school, region and COMIMa centre. This information is only used for coaching; it is never published.

Install in three steps

  1. Download the file from comima.mg/app: the page picks the one that suits your phone (Android 7 or later).
  2. Open it; Android asks to allow installing from the browser. That is normal: the app does not come from a store.
  3. Open COMIMa. Everything is already inside, nothing more to download.

If the install fails with the first file, take another file from the page: the “universal” file works everywhere. Updates are offered by the app itself, and every file is verified before it is installed.

An account, if you wish

Accounts (Google, Facebook or e-mail) are optional: without one, everything works. With an account, your progress follows you to another phone, and to the website: the Training page offers the same quizzes, graded by the same engine, and the same sign-in. What you do on one shows up on the other.

What’s next?

Follow-up by your coach comes next, along with exercise suggestions from coaches, with their solution, to enrich the library. A question that looks wrong or unclear? Tell your coach: that is how the app gets better.

Happy practising!
