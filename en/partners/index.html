Our partners and associations

COMIMa does not walk alone: a national association at the centre, partners and sponsors who support it, students who help the coaches, and local associations that bring each centre to life.

The heartPartners and sponsorsSupport for coachesCentre associations

The heart

The national association around which everything is organised.

COMIMa

National association

COMIMa, the Mathematics and Computer Science Olympiad Club of Madagascar, is the heart of the network: the national association that runs the camps, the group sessions and the resources, and brings the centres together.

Partners and sponsors

Those who support the club: guidance, funding, technical help.

Animath

Partner

French mathematics outreach association and partner of COMIMa. Animath sends representatives to the club, especially during the national camps.

Discover →

Campus France

Sponsor

French agency for higher education and the reception of international students. Campus France funded the national camp and will also support the following camps.

Discover →

Fianarako

Partner, possible collaborator

Sharing platform and place of support for teacher-creators, promoting and supporting Malagasy educators through e-learning. Partner of COMIMa and possible collaborator: technical support and indirect funding.

Discover →

APTICMAD

Partner

Partner of COMIMa, at its side since the July camp: logistics and teaching equipment.

Support for coaches

The students who lend a hand to the coaches.

AEMIA

Antananarivo students’ association

Association of Mathematics and Computer Science Students of Antananarivo. AEMIA students help the coaches run the sessions and group gatherings.

Discover →

Centre associations

Independent local associations that bring COMIMa’s centres to life.

MahKinga

Antsiranana — independent affiliate

MahKinga Maths-CS Club: an indirect affiliate of COMIMa in Antsiranana, yet independent. It runs the Antsiranana centre.

Discover →

iMatematiko

Fianarantsoa — independent association

Mathematics club of the École Normale Supérieure de Fianarantsoa. Independent association, co-organiser and partner of the Fianarantsoa centre.

Discover →
Tomorrow, one association per centre

Each centre will soon have its own local association, on the model of MahKinga in Antsiranana. For now, the other centres have not created one yet. In Fianarantsoa a local association will also be born; it will be distinct from iMatematiko, which remains an independent partner association.
