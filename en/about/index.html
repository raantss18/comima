About COMIMa

Our mission

COMIMa — the Mathematics and Computer Science Olympiad Club of Madagascar — prepares Malagasy middle and high school students for mathematics and computer science competitions: national olympiads, Pan-African (PAMO) and international ones (IMO, IOI).

COMIMa is a national association, present in five centres: Antananarivo, Antsiranana, Ambositra, Ambatondrazaka and Fianarantsoa. Sessions are free and open to all motivated students, whatever their school.

Beyond competitions, our goal is to share the joy of mathematical research: searching for a long time, failing, trying again, and savouring a beautiful solution.

Our partners

COMIMa is the heart of a network: Animath and Campus France support us, Fianarako backs us, AEMIA helps our coaches, and independent local associations such as MahKinga (Antsiranana) and iMatematiko (Fianarantsoa) bring the centres to life.
