Algebraic games and forced choices

In a coefficient game, evaluate the polynomial at a simple value such as $1$ or $-1$, where coefficients have simple weights. The last player can sometimes force a root by choosing one coefficient.

A decimal integer has the same residue modulo $9$ as its digit sum. Choosing the final digit can avoid a single forbidden class.

In Euclid’s game, subtract a positive multiple of one pile from the other without making either negative. Emptying a pile wins. If the larger size is a multiple of the smaller, win immediately. Quotient and remainder describe other moves. Check terminal conditions before invoking a strategy.
