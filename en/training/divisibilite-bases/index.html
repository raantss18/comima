Divisibility, Euclidean division

For an integer $a$ and $b \ge 1$, there is a unique pair $(q, r)$ with $a = bq + r$ and $0 \le r < b$.

The number $q$ is the quotient and $r$ the remainder. An integer $d \ge 1$ divides $a$ (written $d \mid a$) when the remainder of the division of $a$ by $d$ is zero, that is, when $a = dq$ with $q$ an integer.
