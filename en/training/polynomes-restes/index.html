Remainders, divisibility and integer values

The remainder of $P$ modulo $x-r$ is $P(r)$. Modulo a polynomial of degree $m$, the remainder has degree less than $m$. Modulo $x^2-1$, replace $x^2$ by $1$, or determine $ax+b$ by evaluating at $1$ and $-1$.

For an integer-coefficient polynomial and integers $u,v$, $u-v$ divides $P(u)-P(v)$: every $u^k-v^k$ contains that factor. Values modulo $m$ depend only on the argument modulo $m$.

For a double root $r$, check $P(r)=P'(r)=0$. For partial fractions at distinct poles, multiply by the denominator and evaluate at each pole.
