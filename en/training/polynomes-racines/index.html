Roots, Vieta and multiplicity

A zero $r$ satisfies $P(r)=0$. For $x^2-Sx+T$, the two complex roots counted with multiplicity have sum $S$ and product $T$ (Vieta). Hence $r_1^2+r_2^2=S^2-2T$.

A root is double if $(x-r)^2$ divides $P$; over the reals or complexes, this means $P(r)=P'(r)=0$. A nonzero polynomial of degree $m$ has at most $m$ distinct roots. Complete the square to minimize a quadratic.

Powers of quadratic roots satisfy the recurrence obtained by multiplying the root equation by a power of the root. Establish initial values before using it.
