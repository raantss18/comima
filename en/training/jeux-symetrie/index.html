Symmetry and pairing

A pairing strategy matches cells in pairs. After the opponent moves, respond on the paired cell. Prove that it is free and that the response obeys every rule, including piece attacks.

A knight moves by $(\pm2,\pm1)$ or $(\pm1,\pm2)$ and changes cell color. A bishop remains on the same color. A board symmetry is therefore not automatically a legal strategy.

For a path with no revisits on a grid, a domino matching provides an adjacent response: after the opponent enters an untouched domino, leave through its other cell. In the mark/cover game, B may choose any legal domino: A fixing a tiling does not force B to follow it.
