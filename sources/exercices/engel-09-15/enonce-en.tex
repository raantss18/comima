\emph{The arithmetic-geometric mean of Gauss.} Let $0 < a < b$. We define the two sequences $a_n$ and $b_n$ as follows.
\[ a_0 = a,\quad b_0 = b,\qquad a_{n+1} = \sqrt{a_n b_n},\quad b_{n+1} = \frac{a_n + b_n}{2}. \]
(a) Prove that $a_n < a_{n+1}$, $b_n > b_{n+1}$ and $a_n < b_n$ for all $n$.

(b) Prove that $b_{n+1} - a_{n+1} = (b_n - a_n)^2/8b_{n+2}$.

(c) Show that $\lim_{n\to\infty} a_n = \lim_{n\to\infty} b_n = g$ with a quadratic convergence rate.
