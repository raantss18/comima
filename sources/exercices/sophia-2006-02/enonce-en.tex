Consider $18$ points in the plane, $6$ red, $6$ green, $6$ blue, no three collinear. Let $\mathcal{A}$ be the sum of the areas of the triangles formed by these points, and $\mathcal{B}$ the sum for the monochromatic triangles. Show that $4\mathcal{B} \leq \mathcal{A}$.
