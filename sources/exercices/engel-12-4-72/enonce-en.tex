Consider the following theorems:

$(U)$: \emph{Circumscribed quadrilateral} $\iff \alpha + \gamma = \beta + \delta = 180^\circ$.

$(I)$: \emph{Inscribed quadrilateral} $\iff a + c = b + d$.

$(A)$: \emph{Area of quadrilateral} $A = \sqrt{abcd}$.

\emph{Prove that} $(U), (I) \Rightarrow (A)$, $\quad (U), (A) \Rightarrow (I)$, $\quad (I), (A) \Rightarrow (U)$.
