\textbf{Problem 2.6 (Dujella, 2026).} Are there infinitely many elliptic curves over $\mathbb{Q}$ with torsion group $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$ (respectively $\mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z}$) and rank $\geqslant 6$?

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} There are infinitely many curves with rank $\geqslant 5$ and torsion groups $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z}$ (Eroshkin (2009)).
