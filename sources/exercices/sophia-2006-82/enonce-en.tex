% English translation (COMIMa) of the original (French) statement.
Let $A$ be a set with $n$ elements. Some $3$-element subsets $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_k$ are chosen such that for all $i \neq j$, $A_i$ and $A_j$ have at most one element in common. Show that there is a subset of $A$ with at least $\sqrt{2n}$ elements containing none of the $A_i$.
