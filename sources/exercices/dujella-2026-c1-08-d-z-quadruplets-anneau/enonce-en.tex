\textbf{Problem 1.8 (Dujella, 2026).} Characterize elements $z$ of the ring $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-2}]$ for which there exists a $D(z)$-quadruple.

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} Partial results on (non)existence of $D(z)$-quadruples in $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-2}]$ were obtained by Abu Muriefah and Al-Rashed (Math. Commun. \textbf{9} (2004), 1--8), Franušić (Math. Commun. \textbf{9} (2004), 141--148), Dujella and Soldo (An. Ştiinţ. Univ. ``Ovidius'' Constanţa Ser. Mat. \textbf{18} (2010), 81--98), et Soldo (Miskolc Math. Notes \textbf{14} (2013), 265--277).
