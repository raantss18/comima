A polynomial $f(x) = x^4 + {*}x^3 + {*}x^2 + {*}x + 1$ has three undetermined coefficients denoted by stars. The players $A$ and $B$ move alternately, replacing a star by a real number until all stars are replaced. $A$ wins if all zeros of the polynomial are complex. $B$ wins if at least one zero is real. Show that $B$ can win in spite of his only second move.
