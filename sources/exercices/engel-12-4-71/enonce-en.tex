Let $P$ be an arbitrary point inside $\triangle ABC$; $x$, $y$, $z$ the distances of $P$ from $A$, $B$, $C$, respectively; $u$, $v$, $w$ the distances from the sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$, respectively. The sides of $ABC$ will be denoted by $a$, $b$, $c$, its area by $S$; $R$ and $r$ are the radii of circumscribed and inscribed circles. Prove the following inequalities:

(a) $ax + by + cz \geq 4S$; (b) $x + y + z \geq 2(u + v + w)$;

(c) $xu + yv + zw \geq 2(uv + vw + wu)$.
