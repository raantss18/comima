$n$ numbers are written on a blackboard. In one step you may erase any two of the numbers, say $a$ and $b$, and write, instead $(a + b)/4$. Repeating this step $n - 1$ times, there is one number left. Prove that, initially, if there were $n$ ones on the board, at the end, a number, which is not less than $1/n$ will remain.
