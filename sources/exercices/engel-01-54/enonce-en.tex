Initially, we have three piles with $a$, $b$, and $c$ chips, respectively. In one step, you may transfer one chip from any pile with $x$ chips onto any other pile with $y$ chips. Let $d = y - x + 1$. If $d > 0$, the bank pays you $d$ dollars. If $d < 0$, you pay the bank $|d|$ dollars. Repeating this step several times you observe that the original distribution of chips has been restored. What maximum amount can you have gained at this stage?
