A point $P_0$ and a triangle $A_1A_2A_3$ are given in a plane. Let us set $A_s = A_{s-3}$ for all $s \geq 4$. We construct the sequence $P_0, P_1, P_2, \ldots$ of points, so that the point $P_{k+1}$ is the map of $P_k$ rotated around $A_{k+1}$ by $120^\circ$ clockwise (mathematically negative sense) $(k = 0, 1, 2, \ldots)$. Show that if $P_{1986} = P_0$, then triangle $A_1A_2A_3$ is regular (IMO 1986).
