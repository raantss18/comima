Let $ABC$ be a triangle. Let $I$ and $I_A$ be the centres of the incircle of $ABC$ and of the excircle opposite $A$. The perpendicular bisector of $[II_A]$ meets $(AB)$ (resp. $(AC)$) at $L$ (resp. $N$). Show that if $(LN)$ is tangent to the circumcircle of $ABC$, then $ABC$ is isosceles.
