Consider all lattice squares $(x, y)$ with $x, y$ nonnegative integers. Assign to each its lower left corner as a label. We shade the squares $(0, 0)$, $(1, 0)$, $(0, 1)$, $(2, 0)$, $(1, 1)$, $(0, 2)$. (a) There is a chip on each of the six squares (b) There is only one chip on $(0, 0)$.

\emph{Step}: If $(x, y)$ is occupied, but $(x + 1, y)$ and $(x, y + 1)$ are free, you may remove the chip from $(x, y)$ and place a chip on each of $(x + 1, y)$ and $(x, y + 1)$. The goal is to remove the chips from the shaded squares. Is this possible in the cases (a) or (b)? (Kontsevich, TT 1981.)
