\textbf{Problem 5.11 (Dujella, 2026).} Is there any non-zero integer $n$ and a $D(n)$-sextuple with all odd elements?

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} Petričević (2018) found examples of $D(n)$-quintuples with all odd elements: $\{-273375,-361375,-504063,833,1377\}$ is a $D(831406275)$-quintuple and $\{-9,59,6075,47291,555579\}$ is a $D(5117175)$-quintuple.
