Three automata $I$, $R$, $S$ print pairs of positive integers on tickets. For entry $(x, y)$, the automata $I$, $R$, $S$ give tickets $(x - y, y)$, $(x + y, y)$, $(y, x)$, respectively, as outputs. Initially, we have the ticket $(1, 2)$. With these automata, can I get the tickets (a) $(19, 79)$ (b) $(819, 357)$? Find an invariant. What pairs $(p, q)$ can I get starting with $(a, b)$? Via which pair should I best go?
