\textbf{Problem 1.3 (Dujella, 2026).} Let $\{a,b,c\}$ be a $D(-1)$-triple and $d$ a positive integer such that $ad+1$, $bd+1$, and $cd+1$ are perfect squares. Do all such quadruples satisfy the formula
\[
(d+a+b-c)^2 = 4(ab-1)(cd+1)\ ?
\]

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} Fujita (Acta Arith. \textbf{128} (2007), 349--375; Rocky Mountain J. Math. \textbf{39} (2009), 1907--1932) and He and Togbé (J. Number Theory \textbf{131} (2011), 120--137) obtained a positive answer for $D(-1)$-triples of the form $\{1,2,c\}$, $\{F_{2k+1}, F_{2k+3}, F_{2k+5}\}$ and $\{1, k^2+1, k^2+2k+2\}$, respectively.
