\textbf{Problem 2.1 (Dujella, 2026).} Are there elliptic curves over $\mathbb{Q}$ of arbitrarily large rank?

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} The current record over $\mathbb{Q}$ is rank at least 29 (Elkies-Klagsbrun, 2024). Heuristics of Park, Poonen, Voight and Wood (2019) suggest that ranks over $\mathbb{Q}$ might be bounded.
