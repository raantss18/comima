\textbf{Problem 5.12 (Dujella, 2026).} Are there infinitely many Diophantine triples that are also $D(n_i)$-triples for $1<n_2<n_3<n_4$?

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} The following triples with the given property were found by Adžaga, Dujella, Kreso and Tadić (2018): $\{4,12,420\}$, $\{10,44,21252\}$, $\{4,420,14280\}$, $\{40,60,19404\}$, $\{78,308,7304220\}$, $\{4,485112,16479540\}$, $\{15,528,32760\}$.
