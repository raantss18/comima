\textbf{Problem 2.9 (Dujella, 2026).} Is there an elliptic curve over a quartic field with torsion group $\mathbb{Z}/22\mathbb{Z}$ and positive rank?

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} Solved by Maksym Voznyy on 2024/01/04. There is a curve with torsion group $\mathbb{Z}/22\mathbb{Z}$ and rank 2 over the quartic field defined by the polynomial $f(x) = x^4 + \frac{50}{27}x^3 + \frac{281}{243}x^2 + \frac{68}{243}x + \frac{2}{81}$. The curve has the equation
\[
y^2 - (201204z^3+328374z^2+159732z+8019)xy - (14996874942306z^3+23680026731268z^2+10746821159640z+931263196176)y
\]
\[
{}= x^3 - (1435244994z^3+2266248132z^2+1028502360z+89124624)x^2,
\]
where $z$ is a root of $f$.
