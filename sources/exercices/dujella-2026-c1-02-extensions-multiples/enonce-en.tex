\textbf{Problem 1.2 (Dujella, 2026).} Is there any positive integer $k \geqslant 2$ such that the Diophantine quadruple $\{k-1, k+1, 4k, 16k^3-4k\}$ has more than one extension to a rational Diophantine quintuple?

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} Stoll (Acta Arith. \textbf{190} (2019), 239--261) proved that the extension is unique for $k = 2, 3, 4, 5$.
