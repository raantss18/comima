\textbf{Problem 3.1 (Dujella, 2026).} Is there any rational Diophantine sextuple with at least two integer elements?

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} There are infinitely many rational Diophantine sextuples with at least one integer element. One such sextuple is $\{34440, 23001/280, 840/68921, 1121/11480, 19383/1640, 2530/287\}$ found by Piezas (2019).
