$A$ and $B$ alternately move a knight on a $1994 \times 1994$ chessboard. $A$ makes only horizontal moves $(x, y) \mapsto (x \pm 2, y \pm 1)$, $B$ makes only vertical moves $(x, y) \mapsto (x \pm 1, y \pm 2)$. $A$ starts by choosing a square and making a move. Visiting a square for a second time is not permitted. The loser is the one who cannot move. Prove that $A$ has a winning strategy (ARO 1994).
