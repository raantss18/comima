Let $f$ be a function from $\mathbb{N}^*$ to $\mathbb{N}^*$ such that $f(mn) = f(m) + f(n) - 1$ for all integers $m$ and $n$. Assume that $f(1980 \times 2002) = 11$ and that there are finitely many $n$ with $f(n) = 1$.

Compute $f(16!)$.
