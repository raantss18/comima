Circles with centers $O$ and $O'$ are disjoined. A tangent from $O$ to the second circle intersects the first circle in $A$ and $B$. A tangent from $O'$ to the first circle intersects the second circle in $A'$ and $B'$, and $A$ and $A'$ lie on the same side of $OO'$. Suppose we know the distances $|AA'| = a$ and $|BB'| = b$. Find $|OO'|$.
