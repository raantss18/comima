\emph{The following problems treat the number $a_n$ of ways to tile a $k \times n$ rectangle by various smaller tiles. A solution is here a recurrence for $a_n$.}

Let $a_n$ be the number of ways to tile a $2 \times n$ rectangle by $2 \times 1$ dominoes. (a) Find $a_n$. (b) Find the number of symmetric and distinct tilings.
