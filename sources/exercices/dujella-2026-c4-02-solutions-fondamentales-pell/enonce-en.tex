\textbf{Problem 4.2 (Dujella, 2026).} Is there any positive integer $k$, $k \neq 33539$, such that the Pellian equation
\[
x^2-(k^2-1)y^2=2-k^2
\]
has more than four fundamental solutions?

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} For $k=33539$ there are six fundamental solutions: $(\pm 1,1)$, $(\pm 669941,20)$, $(\pm 4326401,129)$.
