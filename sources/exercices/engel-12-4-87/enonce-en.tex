The circles $C_1$ and $C_2$ with centers $O_1$ and $O_2$ intersect in the points $A$ and $B$. The ray $O_1B$ intersects $C_2$ in $F$, and the ray $O_2B$ intersects $C_1$ in $E$. The straight line through $B$ and parallel to $EF$ intersects the circles $C_1$ and $C_2$ a second time in $M$ and $N$, respectively. Prove that $MN = AE + AF$.
