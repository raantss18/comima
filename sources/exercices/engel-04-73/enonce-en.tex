The Sikinian road system is such that three roads meet at each intersection. Prove the following property of the Sikinian road system: Start at any intersection $A_1$, and drive along any of the three roads to the next intersection $A_2$. At $A_2$ turn right and go to the next intersection $A_3$. At $A_3$ turn left, and so on, turning left and right alternately. Then you will eventually return to your starting point $A_1$.
