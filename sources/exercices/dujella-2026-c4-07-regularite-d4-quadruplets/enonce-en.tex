\textbf{Problem 4.7 (Dujella, 2026).} Are all $D(4)$-quadruples regular?

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} A positive answer to Problem 4.7 would imply a positive answer to Problem 4.6, while a negative answer to Problem 4.6 would imply a negative answer to Problem 4.7. Namely, $\{a,b,c,d\}$ is a regular Diophantine quadruple if and only if $\{2a,2b,2c,2d\}$ is a regular $D(4)$-quadruple.
