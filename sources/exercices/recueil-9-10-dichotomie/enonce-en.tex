% English translation (COMIMa) of the original (French) statement.
Let $f(x)=-x^3+x^2+x+1$. Write \texttt{dichotomie(debut, fin, precision)} to return a root bracket of width at most \texttt{precision}. Assume \texttt{debut < fin}, \texttt{precision > 0}, $f$ continuous and strictly monotone on the interval, and $f(\texttt{debut})f(\texttt{fin})<0$. If a midpoint is an exact root, return that midpoint twice.

The requested precision must remain attainable with the floating-point numbers used.
