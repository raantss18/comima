Four integers $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$, not all equal, are given. From the quadruple $(a, b, c, d)$ we form $(a', b', c', d')$ with $a' = a - b$, $b' = b - c$, $c' = c - d$ and $d' = d - a$. The operation is repeated from $(a', b', c', d')$, and so on, any number of times. Show that after a while one of the components of the quadruple obtained will be greater than $20062007$.
