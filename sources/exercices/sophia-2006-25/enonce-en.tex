Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle. Let $A_1$ (resp. $C_1$) be the reflection of $A$ (resp. $C$) in $(BC)$ (resp. $(AB)$). Show that if $A_1$, $B$ and $C_1$ are collinear and $C_1B = 2A_1B$, then the angle $\widehat{CA_1B}$ is a right angle.
