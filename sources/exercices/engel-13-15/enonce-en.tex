In the equation $x^3 + \cdots x^2 + \cdots x + \cdots = 0$, $A$ replaces one of the three dots by an integer unequal to $0$. Then $B$ replaces one of the remaining dots by an integer. Finally $A$ replaces the last dots by an integer. Prove that $A$ can play so that all three roots of the resulting cubic equation are integers.
