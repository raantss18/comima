\textbf{Problem 2.4 (Dujella, 2026).} Are there infinitely many elliptic curves over $\mathbb{Q}$ with the torsion group $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z}$ (respectively $\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$) and rank $\geqslant 4$?

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} There are infinitely many curves with rank $\geqslant 3$ and torsion groups $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z}$ (Dujella \& Peral (2013), Dujella, Kazalicki \& Peral (2021)) and $\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$ (Dujella \& Peral (2012), Dujella, Kazalicki \& Peral (2021)).
