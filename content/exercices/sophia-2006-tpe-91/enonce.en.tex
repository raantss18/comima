Let $ABC$ be an equilateral triangle with side $a$. Let $\mathcal{C}_A$ be the circle lying in the perpendicular bisector plane of $[BC]$, centred at the midpoint of $[BC]$, with a fixed radius $r$. Define $\mathcal{C}_B$ and $\mathcal{C}_C$ likewise on the sides $[AC]$ and $[AB]$. Let $A'$ be a point of $\mathcal{C}_A$ such that there are two points $B'$ and $C''$ of $\mathcal{C}_B$ at distance $a$ from $A'$. Let $C'$ be the point of $\mathcal{C}_C$ symmetric to $C''$ with respect to the perpendicular bisector plane of $[BC]$. Show that $A'B'C'$ is an equilateral triangle.
