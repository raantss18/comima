A $25 \times 25$ table is filled with numbers equal to $1$ or $-1$. For every $i$, let $a_i$ be the product of the entries of the $i$-th row and $b_i$ the product of the entries of the $i$-th column. Show that $a_1 + b_1 + \cdots + a_{25} + b_{25}$ is non-zero.
