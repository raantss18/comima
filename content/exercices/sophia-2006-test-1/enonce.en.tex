We have two tarot decks, one with a blue back and the other with a red back. They are shuffled separately, then the red deck is put on top of the blue deck. For each red card, we count the number of cards separating it from its counterpart in the blue deck (the two end cards are not counted). What is the sum of all the numbers thus obtained, minus $4000$?

(Note from the booklet: decks of $78$ cards.)
