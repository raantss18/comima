Let $x_i > 0$, $x_1 + \cdots + x_n = 1$, and let $s$ be the greatest of the numbers
\[ \frac{x_1}{1 + x_1},\ \frac{x_2}{1 + x_1 + x_2},\ \frac{x_3}{1 + x_1 + x_2 + x_3},\ \ldots,\ \frac{x_n}{1 + x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + x_n}. \]
Find the smallest value of $s$. For which $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ will it be assumed?
