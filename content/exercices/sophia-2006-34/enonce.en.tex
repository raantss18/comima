Let $ABC$ be a triangle. Let $H_A$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$, $\mathcal{C}_A$ the circle with diameter $[AH_A]$, and $M$ and $N$ the intersections of this circle with the sides of the triangle. Let $L_A$ be the line through $A$ perpendicular to $(MN)$. The lines $L_B$ and $L_C$ are defined in the same way. Show that the three lines $L_A$, $L_B$ and $L_C$ are concurrent.
