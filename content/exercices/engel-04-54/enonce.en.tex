Let $a_n$ be the number of terms in the sequence $2^1, 2^2, \ldots, 2^n$, which begin with digit $1$. Prove that
\[ \log 2 - \frac{1}{n} < \frac{a_n}{n} < \log 2 \]
and, hence,
\[ p_1 = \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{a_n}{n} = \log 2 \approx 0.30103. \]
One sometimes says that a \emph{randomly chosen} power of two begins with $1$ with probability $\log 2 \approx 0.30103$.
