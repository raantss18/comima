Let $ABCDEF$ be a convex hexagon such that $AB \parallel DE$, $BC \parallel EF$ and $CD \parallel AF$. Let $R_A$, $R_C$, $R_E$ denote the circumradii of triangles $FAB$, $BCD$, $DEF$, respectively, and let $P$ denote the perimeter of the hexagon. Prove that
\[ R_A + R_C + R_E \geq \frac{P}{2} \qquad \text{(IMO 1996).} \]
