On suppose $p$ et $q$ premiers entre eux. Alors
\[ \left\lfloor \frac{p}{q} \right\rfloor + \cdots + \left\lfloor \frac{(q - 1)p}{q} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{q}{p} \right\rfloor + \cdots + \left\lfloor \frac{(p - 1)q}{p} \right\rfloor = \frac{(p - 1)(q - 1)}{2}. \]
