Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB > BC$. Let $M$ and $L$ be the points of $(AC)$ such that $(BM)$ is a median of the triangle and $(BL)$ a bisector. The parallel to $(AB)$ (resp. $(BC)$) through $M$ (resp. $L$) meets $(BL)$ (resp. $(BM)$) at $D$ (resp. $E$). Show that $(ED)$ is perpendicular to $(BL)$.
