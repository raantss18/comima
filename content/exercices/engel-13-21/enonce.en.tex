Initially there is a chip at the corner of an $n \times n$-chessboard. $A$ and $B$ alternately move the chip one step in any direction. They may not move to a square already visited. The loser is the one who cannot move. (a) Who wins for even $n$? (b) Who wins for odd $n$? (c) Who wins if the chip starts on a square, which is neighbor to a corner square?
