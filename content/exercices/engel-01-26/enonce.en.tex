Three automata $I$, $H$, $T$ print pairs of positive integers on tickets. For input $(a, b)$, $I$ and $H$ give $(a + 1, b + 1)$ and $(a/2, b/2)$, respectively. $H$ accepts only even $a, b$. $T$ needs two pairs $(a, b)$ and $(b, c)$ as input and yields output $(a, c)$. Starting with $(5, 19)$ can you reach the ticket (a) $(1, 50)$ (b) $(1, 100)$? Initially, we have $(a, b)$, $a < b$. For what $n$ is $(1, n)$ reachable?
