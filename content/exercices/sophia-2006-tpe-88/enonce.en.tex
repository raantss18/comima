Let $S$ be a finite set of $n \geq 3$ points in the plane. Assume that the distances between any two points are pairwise distinct, and colour red $r$ segments whose endpoints are chosen among the points of $S$. Let $m$ be the smallest integer greater than or equal to $\frac{2r}{n}$. Show that there is a path made of $m$ red segments of increasing length.
