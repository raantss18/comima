Let $(x, y)$ be a solution $x^2 + xy - y^2 = 1$ in positive integers. Prove that

(a) $\gcd(x, y) = 1$; (b) if $x = y$ then $x = y = 1$; (c) $x \leq y < 2x$;

(d) $(x + y, x + 2y)$ and $(2x - y, -x + y)$ are also solutions. Construct an infinite sequence of solutions, and prove that they comprise all solutions.
