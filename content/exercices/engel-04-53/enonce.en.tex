Prove that there is a power of two, which begins with $6$ nines, that is, there are positive integers $n, k$ such that
\[ 999999 \times 10^k < 2^n < 10^{k+6},\qquad k + \log 999999 < n\log 2 < k + 6. \]
\emph{Hint.} Here $\epsilon = 6 - \log 999999$ and the step length is $\alpha = \log 2$. Similarly, one can show that, for irrational $\log a$, there is a power of $a$ which begins with any prescribed digit sequence.
