\textbf{Problem 1.1 (Dujella, 2026).} Find all extensions of the rational Diophantine quadruple
\[
\left\{ \frac{1}{16}, \frac{33}{16}, \frac{17}{4}, \frac{105}{16} \right\}
\]
to a rational Diophantine quintuple.

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} Known extensions are $\frac{549120}{10201}$, $-\frac{26880}{177241}$. Is there any other possibility?
