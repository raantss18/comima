There are $n$ weights on a table with weights $m_1 > m_2 > \cdots > m_n$ and a two-pan scale. The weights are put on the pans one-by-one. To each weighing we assign a word from the alphabet $\{L, R\}$. The $k$th letter of the word is $L$ or $R$ if the left or right pan outweighs the other, respectively. Prove that any word from $\{L, R\}$ can be realized.
