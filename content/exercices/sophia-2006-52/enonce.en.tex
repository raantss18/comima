Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $D$ a point of $(BC)$ such that $B$, $C$, $D$ are collinear in this order and $CD = CA$. The circumcircle of $ACD$ meets the circle with diameter $[BC]$ at $P$, and $(BP)$ (resp. $(CP)$) meets $(AC)$ (resp. $(AB)$) at $E$ (resp. $F$). Show that $D$, $E$ and $F$ are collinear.
