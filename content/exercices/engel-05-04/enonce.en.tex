Let $R_n$ be the number of ways to place $n$ undistinguishable rooks peacefully on an $n \times n$ chessboard. Moreover, let $H_n$, $Q_n$, $M_n$, $D_n$ be the number of those placings, which are invariant with respect to a half-turn, a quarter-turn, reflection at a diagonal, and reflection at both diagonals. Find formulas for $R_n$, $H_n$, $Q_n$, and find recursions for $M_n$, $D_n$.
