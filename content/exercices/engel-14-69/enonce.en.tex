For every pair of real numbers $a$ and $b$, we consider the sequence $p_n = \lfloor 2\{an + b\} \rfloor$. Here $\{c\}$ is the fractional part of $c$. We call any $k$ successive terms of this sequence a \emph{word}. Is it true that any sequence of zeros and ones of length $k$ is a word of a sequence given by some $a$ and $b$: (a) for $k = 4$; (b) for $k = 5$ (MMO 1993)?
