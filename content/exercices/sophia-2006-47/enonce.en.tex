Let $ABC$ be a triangle. Let $D$ be the point of $[AC]$ such that $AD = AB$, and $E$ the point of $[AD]$ such that $BE = EC$. Let $F$ be the midpoint of the arc $\overset{\frown}{BC}$ of the circumcircle of $ABC$. Show that $B$, $D$, $E$ and $F$ are concyclic.
