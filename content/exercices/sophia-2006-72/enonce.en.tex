% English translation (COMIMa) of the original (French) statement.
The integers $a$ and $b$ are coprime, with $a > b$. Compare the numbers
\[
m = \left[\frac{a}{b}\right] + \left[\frac{2a}{b}\right] + \cdots + \left[\frac{(b-1)a}{b}\right]
\]
and
\[
n = \left[\frac{b}{a}\right] + \left[\frac{2b}{a}\right] + \cdots + \left[\frac{(a-1)b}{a}\right]
\]
where $[x]$ denotes the integer part of the real number $x$.
