\textbf{Problem 4.5 (Dujella, 2026).} Is there any Diophantine quadruple $\{a,b,c,d\}$ such that $a \equiv b \equiv c \equiv d \pmod{k}$ with $k \geqslant 3$?

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} It was shown by Dujella and Saradha (Indag. Math. (N.S.) \textbf{25} (2014), 131--136) that a positive answer to Problem 4.6 would imply a negative answer to Problem 4.5.
