Let $\Gamma$ be a semicircle on a line $\ell$, and $C$ and $D$ points of $\Gamma$. Let $A$ and $B$ be the intersections of the tangents at $C$ and $D$ with $\ell$, and assume that $A$ and $B$ lie on either side of $\Gamma$. Let $E$ be the intersection of $(AC)$ and $(BD)$, and $F$ the projection of $E$ on $\ell$. Show that the line $(FE)$ bisects $\widehat{CFD}$.
