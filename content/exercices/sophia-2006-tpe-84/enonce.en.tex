% English translation (COMIMa) of the original (French) statement.
Let $n > 2$ be an integer. Consider the map $T$:
\[
(x_1, \ldots, x_n) \mapsto \left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{x_2 + x_3}{2}, \ldots, \frac{x_n + x_1}{2}\right).
\]
Show that for every $x = (x_1, \ldots, x_n)$ whose $x_i$ are not all equal, there is an integer $k$ such that $T^k(x)$ has a non-integer component.
