Euler's $\phi$-function of a positive integer $n$ is defined as follows:
\[ \phi(n) = \text{the number of positive integers} \leq n \text{ which are prime to } n. \]
Prove that
\[ \phi(n) = n\prod_{i=1}^m \left(1 - \frac{1}{p_i}\right), \]
where $p_1, p_2, \ldots, p_m$ are all distinct prime divisors of $n$. Use PIE.
