Two positive integers are written on the board. In turn, each trainee comes to the board, looks at the two numbers $a$ and $b$, notes that $a$ is the smaller one, and replaces it by $\frac{ab}{b-a}$. Unfortunately, when Samuel's turn comes, he cannot note that $a$ is the smaller number: the two numbers are equal. Show that the number read by Samuel is an integer.
