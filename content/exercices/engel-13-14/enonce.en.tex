$A$ and $B$ take turns in placing a ``$+$'' sign or a ``$-$'' sign in front of one of the numbers in the sequence $1\ 2\ 3\ 4 \cdots 19\ 20$. After all $20$ signs have been placed, $B$ wins the absolute value of the sum. Find the best strategy for each player. How much does $B$ win if both players use their best strategies?
