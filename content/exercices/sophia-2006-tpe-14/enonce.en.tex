% English translation (COMIMa) of the original (French) statement.
The sequence $(a_n)$ is defined by $a_1 = 2$ and $a_{n+1} = \left[\frac{3}{2}a_n\right]$ for every $n \geq 1$. Show that the sequence $(b_n)$ defined by $b_n = (-1)^{a_n}$ is not eventually periodic.

(Recall that $[x]$ denotes the integer part of $x$.)
