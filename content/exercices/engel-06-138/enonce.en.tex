(a) Let $a_n$ be the last nonzero digit in the decimal representation of the number $n!$. Does the sequence $a_1, a_2, a_3, \ldots$ become periodic after a finite number of steps (USSR proposal for IMO 1991)?

(b) Let $d_n$ be the last nonzero digit of $n!$. Prove that $d_n$ is not periodic, that is, $p$ and $n_0$ do not exist such that $d_{n+p} = d_n$ for all $n \geq n_0$ (USSR proposal for IMO 1983).
