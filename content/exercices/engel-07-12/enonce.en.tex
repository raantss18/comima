Let $x_i, y_i$, $1 \leq i \leq n$ be real numbers such that
\[ x_1 \geq x_2 \geq \cdots \geq x_n \quad\text{and}\quad y_1 \geq y_2 \geq \cdots \geq y_n \qquad \text{(IMO 1975)} \]
Let $z_1, z_2, \ldots, z_n$ be any permutation of $y_1, y_2, \ldots, y_n$. Show that
\[ \sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - y_i)^2 \leq \sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - z_i)^2. \]
