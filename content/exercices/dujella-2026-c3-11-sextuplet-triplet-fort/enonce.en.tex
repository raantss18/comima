\textbf{Problem 3.11 (Dujella, 2026).} Is there any rational Diophantine sextuple which contains a strong Diophantine triple?

\medskip

\textit{Remark.} By the recent paper Dujella, A., Kazalicki, M., Petričević, V.: Rational Diophantine sextuples with strong pair. Rev. R. Acad. Cienc. Exactas Fis. Nat. Ser. A Math. RACSAM \textbf{119}, Article 36 (2025), there are infinitely many rational Diophantine sextuples which contain, e.g., a strong Diophantine pair $\{30464/2223, 22815/5168\}$.
