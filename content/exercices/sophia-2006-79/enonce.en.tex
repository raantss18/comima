Consider a language (that is, a set of words) over a two-letter alphabet $A$ and $B$. The language has no one-letter word. The only two-letter words of the language are $AB$ and $BB$. To build words of the language, one may replace $B$ by a word of the language. How many $n$-letter words does the language contain?
